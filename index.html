Privacy Policy for Sages

Last Updated: September 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information Sages collects, how it is used to provide and personalize the service, which service providers may process it, how it is retained, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Who Operates Sages

Sages is currently operated by an individual developer. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your information, or your account, you can contact us at:

support@sagesapp.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Sages, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Information used to create and manage your account, such as your name, email address and authentication information.
  • Conversations: Messages you send in one-to-one mentor conversations and Council conversations, together with AI-generated responses and related conversation information.
  • Personalization Information: Information created or saved to help Sages understand your situation over time, including Memories, Threads, Goals, Decisions and Reflections.
  • Preferences and Settings: Information such as your timezone, Reflection settings and other application preferences.
  • Subscription and Usage Information: Information about your Free or Premium status, purchase entitlement, feature usage and usage allowances. Sages does not store your full credit or debit card details.
  • Notification Information: Device notification tokens and related information required to send push notifications when notifications are enabled.
  • Support Information: Information you provide when contacting Sages Support, including the subject and content of your support request.
  • Technical Information: Information such as application version, device information, crash reports and diagnostics used to maintain the reliability and security of Sages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve the Sages experience, including to:

  • Create, authenticate and secure your account.
  • Provide one-to-one mentor conversations, Council conversations and other Sages features.
  • Preserve conversation continuity across sessions.
  • Personalize responses using relevant Memories, Threads, Goals, Decisions, Reflections and other context.
  • Generate Reflections, contextual follow-ups and other personalized experiences.
  • Identify information that may be useful to remember, such as an important Memory, Goal, Thread or Decision.
  • Operate Free and Premium usage limits.
  • Verify and synchronize subscription access.
  • Deliver notifications that support features such as Reflections and follow-ups.
  • Detect technical problems, investigate crashes and maintain the reliability of the application.
  • Respond to support requests.

Relevant saved context may be selected and used in later AI interactions when Sages determines that it can help provide a more useful and coherent response.

4. AI Processing and Personalization

Sages uses artificial intelligence to provide mentor responses, Council discussions, Reflections, contextual follow-ups and other personalized functionality.

When you use an AI-powered feature, information necessary to perform that feature may be sent to an AI service provider. This can include the message you submitted and relevant context selected by Sages from information associated with your account.

Sages currently uses OpenAI for AI generation, related AI processing and embeddings used to retrieve relevant context.

We do not intentionally send your account password or full payment-card details to the AI provider.

5. Service Providers

Sages relies on trusted technology providers to operate the service. Depending on the feature being used, information may be processed by the following providers:

  • Supabase: Provides authentication and database infrastructure used to store and manage Sages account and application data.
  • OpenAI: Processes text and relevant context required to provide AI-powered features and embeddings.
  • RevenueCat: Helps Sages manage subscription entitlement status and synchronize purchases made through supported app stores.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: Process purchases and subscriptions made through their platforms. Sages does not receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Firebase: Sages uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash and diagnostic reporting and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push-notification delivery.
  • DigitalOcean: Provides hosting infrastructure used to operate Sages backend services.
  • Inngest: Helps coordinate certain background workflows. Information necessary to perform those workflows may be processed through the service.
  • Email Service Providers: When you contact Sages Support through the application, the information you submit is transmitted through email infrastructure so your request can be received and answered.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and the agreements governing their services.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

Sages may provide both Free and Premium access.

Purchases made through the Sages mobile application are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat is used to help Sages determine whether your account currently has access to Premium features.

Sages does not store your full credit-card or debit-card number.

Cancelling or managing a subscription is handled through the app store where the subscription was purchased.

7. Conversation History, Retention and Deletion

Different types of information in Sages are stored separately because they serve different purposes.

Clearing Conversation History: Clearing your conversation history permanently deletes your saved conversations and their messages.

Memories, Goals, Threads, Decisions and Reflections are stored separately and are not deleted when you clear conversation history. Where one of these records was originally linked to a deleted conversation or message, Sages may remove that source link while preserving the record itself.

Deleting Individual Memories: Existing Memories can be removed individually from Sages where the corresponding control is available.

Account Deletion: You can request deletion of your Sages account from within the app. Account deletion removes the associated Sages authentication account.

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, legal, billing, fraud-prevention, backup or operational purposes.

Deleting your Sages account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Store subscriptions must be managed separately through the applicable store.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by Sages.

However, no internet service, software system or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in your version of Sages, you can:

  • Review and reject proposed Memories.
  • Delete existing Memories individually.
  • Clear your conversation history.
  • Restore eligible purchases.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple or Google.
  • Delete your Sages account.
  • Contact Sages Support with questions about your account or privacy.

10. Children’s Privacy

Sages is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sages will continue to evolve, and we may update this Privacy Policy as the application, its features or its service providers change.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the application.
